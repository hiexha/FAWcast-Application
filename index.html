About this model

How it works

FAWcast is a hierarchical logistic regression trained on . It turns ten inputs into a probability with P = 1 / (1 + e−z), and adds a baseline adjustment for each region and province.

How well it predicts

Use with care

  • An outbreak means BPI reports from 2 or more municipalities. Quiet reporting can hide real infestations.
  • Sudden outbreaks in provinces with no recent reports are hard to forecast a quarter ahead. Run the monthly update after the first month.
  • This is a decision-support baseline. Confirm risk with field scouting.
